Welcome to Lux Central

Lux Central has been serving Midtown Phoenix with honest scratch baking, in-house roasted specialty espresso, and hearty farmhouse comfort for two decades. Located at 4402 N Central Ave right off Camelback Road, Lux is eponymously named for the creative light and warmth its welcoming community provides from morning coffee through evening cocktails.

Scratch Bakery & Pastries

Our bakery operates from four in the morning, hand-rolling European butter croissants, baking golden Dutch babies, and glazing wild berry scones with celebratory sprinkles. Everything is made from scratch with zero preservatives.

Evening Bar & Provisions

As twilight settles over Central Avenue, our counter transitions into an intimate cocktail sanctuary. Savor freshly shaken espresso martinis, smoked desert rye old fashioneds, Prime NY strip steaks, and artisan charcuterie boards.
